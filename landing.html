Questions.

Do credits roll over?
+
Monthly plan credits reset each billing cycle. Credits from packs never expire — they stay in your balance indefinitely.
Can I use content commercially?
+
Yes. All content you generate is yours to use commercially — ads, social, landing pages, wherever.
How realistic are the AI influencers?
+
Very. NB Pro renders at 4K with real skin texture — pores, slight asymmetries, natural lighting. Designed to avoid the plastic "AI glass-doll" look.
NB Pro vs NB2 — what's the difference?
+
NB Pro renders 2K or 4K with top-tier face fidelity — portraits and hero images. NB2 is faster and cheaper (5 cr/image) for quick drafts and iteration.
Can I upload a real photo as reference?
+
Yes — upload up to 3 reference photos during influencer creation. The AI anchors to those photos while applying your prompt's styling on top.
How long does generation take?
+
Images in 30–90 seconds. Influencer creation (portrait + sheet) in 2–4 minutes. Videos in 2–5 minutes depending on clip length and model.
