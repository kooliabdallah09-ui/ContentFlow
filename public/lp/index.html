FAQ

Questions, answered.

Still curious? Email contentflow.web@gmail.com. We reply within one business day.

What counts as a credit?

Credits pay for rendering. A 5-second UGC video at 720p costs about 49 credits, a 10-second one about 92, and a product image 5 to 10. Draft ads for testing hooks start at 21 credits. Captions and social posts are free with every ad you make (3 per ad). Longer or higher-resolution ads cost more, and you see the cost before you render.

Do credits expire?

Subscription credits roll over for one billing cycle, up to twice your monthly amount. If you cancel, unused subscription credits stay in your account for 60 days.

Are the creators real people?

No. Every creator is AI-generated, built to look natural in phone-style videos, and you can preview each one before you render. Ad platforms like Meta and TikTok require AI-generated ads to be labelled, so add the disclosure when you publish.

Can I use the ads on paid campaigns?

Yes. Under our Terms you own the AI-generated output made from your inputs, subject to the terms of the AI providers we use. Check each platform's advertising rules before you publish.

Which product pages work?

Any public product page. We read the name, description and benefits. If a page can't be read, upload photos and write a short brief instead.

What if a render fails?

Credits for a failed render are refunded automatically, so you only pay for ads you can use.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, from your account settings. You keep access until the end of the billing period. Refund eligibility is in our refund policy.
