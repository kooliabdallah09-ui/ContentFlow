⏺ Dodo is a Merchant of Record (like Paddle/Lemon Squeezy) — the 10% covers tax
  handling, compliance, chargebacks. Your real options:

  Option 1 — Stay on Dodo, bake the fee into prices
  Divide your target price by 0.90 so you net the right amount after their cut:

  ┌────────────┬────────────┬──────────────┐
  │    Plan    │ Target net │ Listed price │
  ├────────────┼────────────┼──────────────┤
  │ Starter    │ $19        │ $21          │
  ├────────────┼────────────┼──────────────┤
  │ Pro        │ $49        │ $55          │
  ├────────────┼────────────┼──────────────┤
  │ Agency     │ $149       │ $166         │
  ├────────────┼────────────┼──────────────┤
  │ Enterprise │ $599       │ $666         │
  └────────────┴────────────┴──────────────┘

  Users pay a bit more, but you net exactly what you intended.

  Option 2 — Switch back to Stripe (2.9% + $0.30/transaction)
  Stripe is already integrated in the codebase, just disabled. You'd re-enable
  the Stripe routes and handle tax separately via Stripe Tax (auto, just a
  toggle in the dashboard). Net fees drop from 10% to ~3%.

  Downside: you're responsible for VAT/sales tax compliance yourself (Stripe Tax
   helps but you're still the merchant of record).

  Option 3 — Switch to Polar
  Also already in the codebase. ~4% fee, developer-focused, simpler setup than
  Stripe Tax.

  ---
  My recommendation: switch to Stripe + Stripe Tax. It's already wired in your
  codebase, fees are 3× lower, and Stripe Tax handles VAT automatically. Dodo's
  MoR model is only worth the premium if you're selling globally to
  non-technical buyers who need localized checkout — for a SaaS you control,
  Stripe is better economics.

  Which do you want to do?
