# ContentFlow — Positioning

Last updated: 2026-09-12 (widened ICP from dropshippers-only to e-commerce brands + killed legally-risky "doesn't look AI" phrasing)

## The decision

**Category**: The AI ad engine for e-commerce brands
**Tagline**: Ads that pass review. For stores that ship every week.

Every landing block, pricing page, DM, ad, and case study reinforces one of these. If a piece of copy doesn't help sell the category or the tagline, cut it.

---

## Who we serve — the ICP

Solo and small e-commerce owners running paid social:
- Shopify store owners
- Small DTC brands ($10k-$500k/mo revenue)
- Amazon private-label sellers
- Etsy sellers with real inventory
- TikTok Shop sellers (own product or reselling)
- Small agencies running 1-5 stores
- Dropshippers still welcome but not the exclusive target

Common thread:
- Test 5-30 ad variants a week
- Founder or 1-2 person team
- Budget $19-$149/mo tool spend, hard NO above $250
- Care about margin
- **Fear ad account bans more than any other loss** — this is the load-bearing insight
- Have been burned by credit-based tools before

## Why we widened from dropshippers-only

The previous ICP was too narrow, retention math forced the change:

| Segment | 90-day churn | Avg tenure | LTV per user |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dropshippers only | ~65% | 2-4 months | $25-50 |
| E-commerce brands (broader) | ~25% | 12-24 months | $180-350 |

~7× LTV difference. Brand owners have something to lose, dropshippers don't. Same workflow (URL → ad), same features work, dramatically better business.

## Who we do NOT serve

Being explicit sharpens who we serve. State on the pricing page.

- Filmmakers (Runway serves them)
- Enterprise brand teams and Fortune 500 (Higgsfield, HeyGen, Synthesia)
- L&D / corporate training (Synthesia)
- Agencies managing 50+ clients (they need real API + white-label at scale)
- Users who want a full timeline editor (CapCut, Descript, Filmora)
- Anyone whose primary product isn't a physical thing

## The market reality that informs all of this

Load-bearing facts from research (competitor audit + buyer psychology, Sept 2026):

1. **AI-content disclosure is now regulated.** Meta flagged 13M+ AI ads. TikTok removed 51,618 synthetic videos. New York + EU + FTC all have disclosure requirements. Positioning against disclosure is legal risk.
2. **"Creative velocity is the new moat"** (Nick Theriot). Winners ship 20-50 variants a week.
3. **Nobody serves e-commerce brand owners with review-safe positioning.** Every competitor either targets Fortune 500 (Higgsfield, HeyGen), makes creators evade detection (unclaimed AI-flavored positioning), or is a supplier company (Zendrop).
4. **Credit burn is universal churn driver #1.** Every review site.
5. **Ad account bans are the industry's night terror.** No tool markets against it explicitly.
6. **The stack problem is real.** Operators use 3-4 tools because no one closed the loop.
7. **The $49-79/mo slot is still relatively open** for one-wallet complete-stack outputs — but competitors like Creatify sit in this band.
8. **Trust wedge exists**: AdCreative and Arcads have public Trustpilot billing scandal reputations. Auto-refund on failure is a real differentiator.
9. **Retention advantage of brand owners**: ~3-7× LTV vs. pure dropshippers.

## Success criteria — how we know positioning is working

Track these monthly. Positioning is working when:

- Landing → `/try` conversion > 20% (previously projected 25%, benchmark-adjusted downward)
- `/try` → signup > 15% (benchmark-adjusted)
- Search traffic includes "e-commerce" and "shopify ads" adjacent terms
- Support/DMs mention "passes review" or "ad account" naturally
- Users describe us as "the e-com ads tool" without prompting
- Monthly churn < 15% (benchmark-adjusted from previous 10%)

Positioning is failing when:
- Users compare us to Runway or Synthesia (wrong audience reached)
- CAC creeps above $30 organic
- Users ask for enterprise features
- Churn reasons cite Higgsfield or HeyGen as replacements
