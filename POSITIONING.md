# ContentFlow — Positioning

Last updated: 2026-09-11

## The decision

**Category**: The AI ad engine for dropshippers
**Tagline**: Ads that don't look AI. For stores that ship every week.

Every landing block, pricing page, DM, ad, and case study reinforces one of these. If a piece of copy doesn't help sell the category or the tagline, cut it.

---

## Who we serve — the ICP

Solo dropshippers and one-person e-com stores who:
- Test 10+ ad variants a week
- Sell primarily on TikTok Shop, Shopify, Amazon, TikTok/Meta ads
- Are the founder + operator (not a marketing team)
- Have a $19-$149/mo tool budget and REJECT anything over $200
- Care about margin and unit economics
- Fear ad account bans more than any other loss
- Cannot afford to burn credits on generations they can't use

## Who we do NOT serve

Being explicit about this sharpens who we serve. State on the pricing page.

- Filmmakers (Runway serves them)
- Enterprise brand teams and Fortune 500 (Higgsfield, HeyGen, Synthesia)
- L&D / corporate training (Synthesia)
- Agencies managing 50+ clients (they need real API + white-label at scale)
- Users who want a full timeline editor (CapCut, Descript, Filmora)
- Anyone whose primary product isn't a physical thing you can put in a URL

Great positioning names its enemies. Ours:

1. **The 4-tool stack** — "Stop paying for Arcads + Higgsfield + Creatify + CapCut. One wallet does everything."

3. **AI slop that gets you banned** — "Ads that look like ads." → Counter: platform-safe passing outputs.

## The market reality that informs all of this

Two research streams (competitor audit + buyer psychology, Sept 2026) surfaced these load-bearing facts:

1. **AI is now a negative signal.** UGC works because it doesn't look AI. TikTok removed 51,618 synthetic videos. Meta flagged 13M+ AI ads.
2. **"Creative velocity is the new moat"** (Nick Theriot). Winners ship 20-50 variants a week. Losers ship 5.
3. **Nobody claims "dropshipper" on their homepage.** Zendrop uses the word as a supplier company. Every AI ad tool markets to "marketers," "brands," "creators," or "enterprise."
4. **Credit burn is universal churn driver #1.** Every review site.
5. **Ad account bans from AI content are the industry's night terror.** No tool markets against it.
6. **The stack problem is real.** Operators use 3-4 tools because no one closed the loop.
7. **The $49-79/mo slot is empty** for flat-priced high-quality UGC + dropshipper language + multi-language output.
8. **Trust wedge exists**: AdCreative and Arcads have public Trustpilot billing scandal reputations.

## Pricing — reframing, not restructuring

Current price ladder is correctly placed. What needs to change is the MESSAGING.

| Plan | Price | Old framing | New framing |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 + 30cr signup | 30 credits | "Try one ad on any product URL. No card." |
| Lite | $8 | 300cr | "One product a week. Test the tool." |
| **Starter** | $19 | 800cr | **"Test 20 hooks per month."** |
| **Pro** ⭐ | $49 | 2000cr | **"Ship 20 ads per week."** ← Hero plan. Owns the empty $49-79 slot. |
| Agency | $149 | 6500cr | "Multiple brands. Multiple stores." |
| Enterprise | $605 | 25000cr | **Hide from public pricing.** "Contact us for high-volume" only. |

Add on the pricing page:
- Trust bar: **"Cancel anytime. No hidden fees. Credits never expire."**
- Per-ad cost estimator widget: "Your $49 gets you ~20 UGC ads at 720p or ~10 at 1080p."
- Explicit "Who this isn't for" section at the bottom.

## Anti-positioning statement (for the landing page)

Include a section that says out loud who we're NOT for. Filters weak leads, builds trust with the right ones.

Example copy:
> **This isn't for everyone. Skip us if:**
> - You're a filmmaker (try Runway)
> - You run a Fortune 500 marketing team (try Higgsfield)
> - You do L&D training videos (try Synthesia)
> - You want a full timeline editor (try CapCut)
> - Your product isn't a physical thing with a URL
>
> **This IS for you if:**
> - You run a store, test creatives every week, and hate credit math

## Success criteria — how we know positioning is working

Track these monthly. Positioning is working when:

- Landing → `/try` conversion > 25%
- `/try` → signup > 20%
- Search traffic includes "dropshipper" adjacent terms
- Support/DMs mention the tagline verbatim
- Users describe us as "the dropshipper tool" without prompting
- Churn stays < 10%/month

Positioning is failing when:
- Users compare us to Runway or Synthesia (wrong audience reached)
- CAC creeps up (message isn't resonating)
- Users ask for enterprise features (wrong audience letting through)
- "Made a video with Y instead" churn reasons cite Higgsfield or HeyGen (still fighting wrong battles)

## The one thing to remember

If a decision is unclear — a feature, a price, a landing block, a piece of copy — ask:

**"Does this help a solo dropshipper ship more ads that don't look AI, without paying for 4 tools?"**

If yes, ship it.
If no, cut it.
